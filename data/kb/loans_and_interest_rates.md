## Northwind Personal Loans
Personal loans are offered from ₹50,000 up to ₹25,00,000. Interest rates start at 10.50% per annum for salaried borrowers with credit scores of 750 or higher. Tenures range from 12 months to 60 months. The processing fee is 1.50% of the loan amount, with a minimum of ₹1,000 and maximum of ₹10,000 plus GST.

## Northwind Home Loans
Home loan interest rates start at 8.40% per annum linked to the Northwind External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR). Maximum repayment tenure is 30 years (360 months). Processing fee is 0.50% of the loan amount up to a maximum cap of ₹15,000. Part-prepayment has zero charges for floating rate home loans.

## Northwind Auto and Car Loans
Car loans for new vehicles offer an interest rate of 8.90% per annum for tenures up to 7 years (84 months). Up to 90% on-road financing is available. Processing fee is a flat ₹2,500 plus GST. Used car loans are offered at 12.00% per annum for up to 5 years.

## Foreclosure and Prepayment Terms
Foreclosure charges for fixed-rate personal loans are 3.00% of the outstanding principal balance if closed after 12 months. No foreclosure is permitted within the first 6 months. Floating-rate home and personal loans to individual borrowers have zero foreclosure penalties.
