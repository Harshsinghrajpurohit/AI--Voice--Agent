## Fixed Deposit Interest Rates for Regular Citizens
UCIC Bank offers competitive Fixed Deposit (FD) rates:
- 7 to 45 days: 3.50% per annum.
- 46 to 179 days: 4.75% per annum.
- 180 to 364 days: 5.75% per annum.
- 1 year to under 2 years: 6.80% per annum.
- 2 years to under 3 years: 7.10% per annum.
- 3 years up to 5 years: 6.50% per annum.
The minimum deposit amount for an FD is ₹1,000.

## Senior Citizen FD Interest Rates
Senior citizens aged 60 years and above receive an additional 0.50% interest rate over standard rates across all tenures. The highest FD rate for senior citizens is 7.60% per annum for tenures between 2 years and under 3 years.

## Premature Withdrawal Policy
Premature withdrawal of fixed deposits is allowed subject to a penalty of 1.00% deducted from the applicable interest rate for the period the deposit was held. No interest is payable if the FD is closed within 7 days of booking.

## Recurring Deposit (RD) Schemes
UCIC Recurring Deposits require a minimum monthly installment of ₹500 in multiples of ₹100. Tenures range from 6 months to 10 years. RD interest rates mirror regular FD rates, with a peak rate of 7.10% per annum for a 2-year tenure. Late installment fee is ₹1.50 per ₹100 installment per month.
