## Emergency Debit and Credit Card Blocking
If your debit or credit card is lost, stolen, or compromised, block it immediately through any of these 24/7 channels:
1. SMS: Send `BLOCK <last 4 digits of card>` to 567676 from your registered mobile number.
2. Northwind Mobile App: Go to Cards > Manage Card > Instant Block.
3. 24x7 Toll-Free Fraud Helpline: Call 1800-419-0022.

## Reporting Fraudulent or Unauthorized Transactions
Unauthorized electronic transactions must be reported within 72 hours (3 days) to Northwind Bank under zero-liability protection. Call the 24x7 fraud desk at 1800-419-0022 or email report.fraud@northwindbank.com with the transaction reference ID, date, and amount.

## Net Banking and UPI Limits
- Default Daily UPI Transaction Limit: ₹1,00,000 across a maximum of 20 transactions per 24 hours.
- Net Banking IMPS Limit: Up to ₹5,00,000 per day.
- Net Banking RTGS Limit: Minimum ₹2,00,000 with no upper limit for verified beneficiaries.
- New beneficiary cool-off period: ₹50,000 maximum transfer during the first 24 hours after adding a new payee.
