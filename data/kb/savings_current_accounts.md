## UCIC Classic Savings Account
The UCIC Classic Savings Account offers an interest rate of 4.00% per annum, calculated on daily closing balance and credited quarterly. The minimum average monthly balance (MAB) requirement is ₹5,000 for urban branches and ₹2,500 for semi-urban or rural branches.

## Minimum Balance Penalties for Savings Accounts
If the monthly average balance drops below ₹5,000 in urban branches, a shortfall fee of ₹250 plus 18% GST is charged monthly. For rural branches falling below ₹2,500, the fee is ₹100 plus 18% GST per month.

## UCIC Premium Privilege Savings Account
The Privilege Savings Account offers an interest rate of 4.50% per annum for balances exceeding ₹1,00,000. The required minimum average monthly balance is ₹50,000. It includes complimentary platinum debit cards with zero annual fees and unlimited free ATM transactions at all domestic ATMs.

## UCIC Business Current Account
The UCIC Business Current Account is tailored for enterprises and sole proprietorships. It earns 0.00% interest. The required minimum quarterly average balance (QAB) is ₹25,000. Non-maintenance of QAB incurs a quarterly fee of ₹1,000 plus GST. It includes 100 free cash deposit transactions up to ₹5,00,000 per month.
