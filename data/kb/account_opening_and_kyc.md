## Mandatory KYC Documents for Individuals
Opening an account at UCIC Bank requires two primary identity verifications:
1. Proof of Identity and Address: Aadhaar Card (with masked number) or valid Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License.
2. Tax Identity: Permanent Account Number (PAN) card or Form 60 if PAN is unavailable.
One recent passport-sized photograph is also required for in-branch applications.

## Video KYC (V-KYC) Online Account Opening
Customers can open a full-fledged savings account digitally via Video KYC on the UCIC mobile app or website.
Requirements for Video KYC:
- Original PAN Card ready for physical display to the verification agent.
- A well-lit quiet room with a stable internet connection.
- Active mobile number registered with Aadhaar for receiving OTP authentication.
The Video KYC service is operational from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM Monday through Saturday.

## Dormant and Inactive Accounts
If no customer-initiated transaction occurs in an account for 12 consecutive months, it is designated as Inactive. If inactivity extends to 24 months, the account becomes Dormant. Reactivation requires fresh in-person KYC document submission at any UCIC branch and is completely free of charge.
